* **Android and iPad: a mouse wheel and a touchpad scroll reach widgets**
  ([#1510](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1510)). On Android a
  wheel notch scrolled nothing, and moving a mouse with no button held
  dragged whatever had last been touched, because every mouse event arrived
  as a finger. A touchpad's two-finger scroll (Android 14 and later) dragged
  the widget under the pointer instead of scrolling it. Now a wheel notch
  scrolls as far as it does in the device's other apps, hovering does
  nothing, and a two-finger scroll is a touchpad scroll with its beginning
  and end (`ScrollEvent.Phase`, `event.ScrollEnder`), as on macOS. On an iPad
  the driver read no trackpad or wheel scroll at all; it now reads both
  through UIKit's scroll gestures and delivers them the same way.
