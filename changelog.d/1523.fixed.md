* **An `Entry`'s selection highlight covers its text when the text is not at
  the left** ([#1523](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1523)).
  Right to left, and with a centered or trailing alignment, the highlight was
  drawn from the left edge while the text sat in the middle or on the right:
  selecting the whole text highlighted empty space. The highlight now covers
  the selected text, a partial selection, and each row of a multi-line
  entry, and it follows the text when the entry is resized while text is
  selected. `RichEntry` was not affected.
