* **Scrolling lists of cards cost less CPU to paint**
  ([#599](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/599)). Every frame, the
  paint walk, and under partial repaint the dirty-region walk too, asked each
  off-screen card three questions of its renderer to decide it could be
  skipped. The answers are now remembered per object until it is refreshed or
  the theme changes. On a Pixel 9a with 2,000 cards that saves about 1 ms of
  CPU per frame with full repaint and 1.3 ms with partial repaint.
