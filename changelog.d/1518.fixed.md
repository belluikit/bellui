* **x/tables: a tap on a `DataGrid` header no longer sorts with a drag's
  modifiers** ([#1518](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/pull/1518)). A
  mouse press that ended in a drag on the header left its modifiers for the
  next tap, and a finger's tap, which sends no press of its own, took them:
  after a Shift-drag on the header, a tap added its column to the sort
  instead of sorting by it alone.
