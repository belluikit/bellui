* **Right-to-left, a `Scroll`'s vertical scroll bar is on the left**
  ([#1496](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1496), part of
  [#779](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/779)). This applies to
  every scroller, so also `List`, `Tree`, `TreeView`, `Table`, a scrolling
  `RichText` or multi-line `Entry`, and anything else built on `Scroll`. On
  desktop, the strip a scroller keeps free for the bar (`ReserveBarSpace`)
  moves to the left with it, and the content still ends at the scroller's
  right edge. Flutter and Android place the bar the same way. In a `Table`
  the bar used to sit between the cells and the header column; it is now at
  the table's outer edge. `List`, `Tree`, `TreeView` and a scrolling
  `RichText` lay their rows out across their whole width, under the bar's
  strip, so right-to-left they now reach under it on the left. x/editor's
  hover layer leaves the strip free on the left, so the bar still widens
  under the pointer there. Content of your own that lays itself out across
  the scroller's width rather than its own runs past the right edge by the
  strip under right-to-left; lay it out across its own width. Left-to-right
  is unchanged.
