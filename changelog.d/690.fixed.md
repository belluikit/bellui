* **x/mmedia: a player with audio no longer hangs when the audio device will
  not open; its constructor returns the error**
  ([#690](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/690)). With no audio
  device (a headless machine or CI runner) or a device that refused the
  format, the first player with sound blocked forever, and every later one
  behind it. `New*Player`, `New*PlayerURL` and `NewCapturePlayer*` now return
  the error. The device is opened once per process and oto cannot retry, so
  every later player with audio returns the same first error. A video with no
  audio stream is unaffected.
