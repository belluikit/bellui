* **x/tables: a DataGrid draws exactly what is selected**
  ([#1524](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1524)). With the
  default single-cell selection (and with multi-cell selection), a selected
  cell was drawn only when it was in the first column, which painted its
  whole row; anywhere else, a click selected the cell and nothing showed. Each
  selected cell is now highlighted by itself, in any column and band, in both
  directions. With whole-row selection, a row deselected by a click, or by
  its checkbox, stayed highlighted and its checkbox stayed ticked, because
  the last-clicked cell still counted as selected; it no longer does, and
  `IsSelected` now agrees with `GetSelectedRows` in the row modes.
