* **x/tables: DataGrid rows can be moved from the keyboard and by screen
  readers** ([#1512](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1512)). With
  `RowDragEnabled`, Alt+Up and Alt+Down (Option on a Mac) move the focused
  row one place, and the cursor stays on it. Each row offers "Move up" and
  "Move down" to screen readers, without the one past the first or last row.
  Before, only a mouse drag could move a row.
