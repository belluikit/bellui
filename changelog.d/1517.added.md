* **iOS apps can receive remote push notifications**
  ([#1517](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1517)). The new
  `bellui.PushApp` (iOS only) asks for notification permission and reports
  the answer, registers with APNs and reports the device token as hex, and
  delivers a tapped push's payload to `SetOnPushOpened`, whether the tap
  launched the app or it was running. A tap that arrives before the callback
  is set is held for it. A push that arrives while the app is open goes to
  `SetOnPushReceived`, whose result decides whether iOS shows it; with no
  callback it is shown, as before. Elsewhere the free functions report
  `bellui.ErrPushNotSupported`. The app delegate now stays the notification
  delegate for the app's whole life; a rich local notification used to
  replace it, which also lost a tap that launched the app.
