* **x/tables: DataGrid rows swipe sideways to reveal actions**
  ([#1483](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1483)). Set
  `LeadingSwipeActions` and `TrailingSwipeActions` and a finger swiping a row
  slides it to uncover its buttons, as a mail app's rows uncover Archive or
  Delete; released past half of them, or flicked, the row stays open. A
  `RowSwipeAction` with `FullSwipe` also runs when the row is swiped most of
  the way across. A sideways drag still scrolls a grid wider than the screen
  while it can, and swipes only when it starts at the edge. The whole row
  slides, pinned columns included, and the leading actions are on the right in
  a right-to-left layout. The same actions are each cell's actions for a screen
  reader, and the context menu on a secondary tap or the Menu key lists them
  when the app shows none of its own (`RowSwipeMenuItems` adds them to one it
  does). Trackpad swipes are not in this change: they wait on the desktop
  drivers telling a trackpad from a mouse wheel
  ([#391](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/391)).
