* **x/tables: resizing a column in a `DataGrid` that scrolls sideways**
  ([#1499](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1499)). When the
  columns were wider than the grid, dragging any header border collapsed that
  column to its minimum width, whichever way the drag went. A drag now changes
  the column by its distance, within `MinWidth`, `MaxWidth` and the width of
  the header's text. A column that scrolls may now also be dragged wider than
  the grid, which then scrolls sideways; before, the drag stopped where the
  columns filled the grid. A pinned column still stops where the pinned
  columns fill the grid, since it does not scroll.
