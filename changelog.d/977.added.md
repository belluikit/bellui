* **`widget.WheelPicker`: a wheel of items to spin through**
  ([#977](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/977)), as iOS picker
  wheels and Android's NumberPicker do. A drag or fling spins it, it settles
  with one item centred, and a tap centres an item. It is vertical or
  horizontal, and `Wrap` makes it a ring instead of stopping at the ends.
  `OnChanged` fires once per settled change. Screen readers adjust it by one
  item and read the selected item's label.
