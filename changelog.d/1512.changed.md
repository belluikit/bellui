* **x/tables: a DataGrid moves no rows while it orders them itself**
  ([#1512](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1512)). While a sort,
  a row grouping, a pivot or tree data is active, row reordering is off,
  because a dropped row would jump straight back to where the grid puts it.
  A filter or pagination leaves it on. When a row moves, the selection,
  the keyboard cursor and expanded detail rows now stay on their records;
  they used to stay at the old source positions. `OnRowMoved`'s meaning is
  now documented: the app removes the row at `from` and inserts it at
  `to`'s place.
