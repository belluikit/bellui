* **`BELLUI_WEBGPU_BACKENDS` can no longer abort the app or leave its window
  blank** ([#731](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/731)). A list
  that left out the platform's native backend (Metal on macOS, D3D12 on
  Windows, Vulkan on Linux) made the process abort when it opened a window,
  or on Linux and Windows with only `gl`, opened a window that never drew. Such a list
  now gains the native backend, and a warning names what was added. Unset
  or `all` is unchanged, and a list can still leave out a non-native backend
  that crashes at init, which is what the variable is for.
