* **widget: icon buttons in five sizes**
  ([#915](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/915)).
  `IconButton.ButtonSize` picks one of the Material 3 Expressive sizes, so a
  media player's play button can stand out from the controls around it:
  `IconButtonSizeXSmall` (32 dp, 20 dp icon), `Small` (40/24), `Medium`
  (56/24), `Large` (96/32) and `XLarge` (136/40). The zero value,
  `IconButtonSizeDefault`, takes both sizes from the theme as before, so a
  dense theme stays dense. The corners stay fully round.
