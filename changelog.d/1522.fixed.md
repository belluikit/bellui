* **x/tables: a DataGrid edit ends when you click elsewhere, and its editor
  stays inside the body while you scroll**
  ([#1522](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1522)). Clicking
  another cell, the header, a column border or anything outside the grid now
  ends the edit and keeps what was typed, as moving to another cell by
  double-click already did. Switching to another app keeps the edit open, and
  Escape still cancels. While scrolling, the editor used to be drawn over the
  header, the pinned columns and the status bar until its whole cell had left
  the body; it is now cut off at the body's edges, and clicks there reach
  what is drawn. The built-in text and date editors now create a
  `cell.Entry`, a `widget.Entry` that reports losing focus; an app's own
  editor factory can return one to get the same behaviour.
