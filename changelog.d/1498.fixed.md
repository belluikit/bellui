* **x/tables: the checkbox column of a `DataGrid` is a real column**
  ([#1498](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1498)). With
  `Options.CheckboxSelection`, every column's header and floating filter sat
  32 px to the left of its cells, a tap on the right part of a cell landed on
  the next column, and when the grid scrolled sideways the last 32 px of the
  last column could not be scrolled into view. The checkbox column is now the
  first column of the scrolling band: headers, filters, taps, the resize
  borders and the content width all count it, and it scrolls sideways with the
  other columns. With `SelectionMultiRow`, its header holds a select-all box
  that selects every displayed row, or clears them all. A tap on a row's
  checkbox still selects the row as before, but no longer reports a cell click
  through `OnCellClicked`.
