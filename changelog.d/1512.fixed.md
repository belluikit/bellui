* **x/tables: hiding or moving a DataGrid column no longer hands its cells to
  another column** ([#1512](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1512)).
  A visible cell was reused by its row and column alone, and pooled under
  whatever column then stood there. A column whose cells are another kind of
  object (a custom `CellRenderer`, or a grip column) could then give its
  cell to a text column, whose renderer panicked on it. Cells are now reused
  and pooled only as the type they were made for.
