* **x/mmedia: a song is reported finished when it has finished playing**
  ([#882](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/882)). The end of an
  audio-only track came 0.2 to 0.3 s before its sound did, with the position
  at about 2.7 s of a 3 s track, so moving on to the next track at the end cut
  that much off. A seek made after the decoder had read to the end of the
  file, about the last second or two of a track, also ended the song at once;
  that part was fixed with #872 and now has its test.
