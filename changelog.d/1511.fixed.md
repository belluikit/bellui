* **x/tables: a click on a `DataGrid` leaves no row drag or column resize
  behind** ([#1511](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1511)). A
  mouse click on a row (with `Options.RowDragEnabled`), on a header or on a
  column border armed a row drag, column move or resize that only the end of
  a drag cleared, and a click has none. The next drag finished it, and on a
  screen with a mouse and a touchscreen that drag could be a finger
  scrolling the grid: it did not scroll, and the clicked row moved to where
  the finger lifted, or the column was resized. The release of a click now
  disarms what the press armed; mouse drags move rows and columns and resize
  as before.
