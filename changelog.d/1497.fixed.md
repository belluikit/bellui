* **Right-to-left, `Table` mirrors**
  ([#1497](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/pull/1497), part of
  [#779](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/779)). The header column
  and sticky columns are at the right, column 0 next to them, and an
  overflowing table opens there instead of scrolled to its last columns.
  Taps, header-divider drags and the arrow keys follow the mirrored layout.
  `ScrollToOffset`'s X counts from the right edge. Left-to-right is
  unchanged.
