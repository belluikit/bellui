* **Right-to-left, sliders and progress bars run from the right**
  ([#1490](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/pull/1490), part of
  [#779](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/779)). A horizontal
  `Slider`, `RangeSlider`, `ProgressBar` and `ProgressBarInfinite` kept their
  left-to-right shape. Now the minimum is at the right end and a bar fills
  leftwards. A click maps to the mirrored track, and the Left arrow raises a
  slider's value. Wrap a widget in `container.DirectionScope` with
  `DirectionLTR` to keep it left-to-right, as a media scrubber may want.
  Left-to-right layouts are unchanged.
