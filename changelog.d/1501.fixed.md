* **x/tables: right-to-left, `DataGrid` mirrors**
  ([#1501](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/pull/1501), part of
  [#779](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/779)). Column 0 is at the
  right, pinned-left columns at the right edge and pinned-right ones at the
  left. A grid wider than its window opens at column 0 instead of scrolled
  away from it. Taps, hovers, header resize drags, column moves, disclosure
  taps, a row's select checkbox and the arrow keys follow the mirrored
  layout, and so do the checkbox column with its select-all box, the
  floating filters, group rows, row details, the inline editor and a swiped
  row.
  Left-to-right is unchanged.
