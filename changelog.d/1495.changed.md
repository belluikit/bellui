* **Right-to-left, 15 more theme icons mirror, following Material**
  ([#1495](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/pull/1495), part of
  [#779](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/779)). An icon now
  mirrors when Material Symbols marks the same symbol `autoMirrored`. That
  adds copy, redo, undo, list, login, logout, send, help, question, the three
  volume icons, receipt-long, file and file-text to the arrows, chevron,
  reply and forward that already mirrored. Media controls keep their
  orientation.
