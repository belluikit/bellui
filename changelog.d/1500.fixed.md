* **x/tables: a `DataGrid`'s status bar shows from the start**
  ([#1500](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1500)). With
  `Options.ShowStatusBar`, the bar was an empty strip, with no background and
  no row count, until something refreshed the grid (new data, a sort, a
  filter). It now draws its row count as soon as the grid is shown.
