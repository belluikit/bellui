* **List, Table, GridWrap, Tree and TreeView no longer keep renderers for
  objects they stop using** ([#722](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/722)).
  Each one builds an item template only to measure it, on every Refresh, and
  List, Table and Tree drop or hide separators they no longer need. The
  renderers of those objects stayed cached until the cache's one-minute
  expiry, and for the life of the process in a test or benchmark without a
  driver, where `test.DestroyRenderers` could not reach them. The template is
  now released right after it is measured, and dropped separators release
  their renderers.
