* **A trackpad pinch reaches widgets in the browser**
  ([#391](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/391)). In a web build,
  pinching on a trackpad over a `Pinchable` widget such as a chart now pinches
  it, in Chrome and Safari, and so does ctrl+wheel. Browsers send a pinch as
  ctrl+wheel, and the steps multiply back to the browser's own pinch scale; a
  100 px mouse notch zooms by a tenth. Elsewhere ctrl+wheel still scrolls.
