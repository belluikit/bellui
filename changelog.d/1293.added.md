* **A table's columns and rows can be resized on a touch screen**
  ([#1293](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1293)). Long-press a
  header divider, then drag: the column or row follows the finger, as it
  follows a mouse dragging the divider. A finger never hovers, so a touch
  had no way to find the divider. A plain swipe over the header still
  resizes nothing. A long-press elsewhere on a header taps what is under it,
  so a slow click on a button in a header still presses it.
