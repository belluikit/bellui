* **Right-to-left, a selected `List` row's accent bar is on the right**
  ([#1506](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1506), part of
  [#779](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/779)). The short bar the
  modern look draws beside a selected row stayed at the row's left edge,
  away from the right-aligned text it marks. It is now at the leading edge
  in both directions. Left-to-right is unchanged.
