* **`widget.Carousel` pages with a swipe**
  ([#688](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/pull/688)). It documented snapping
  but ignored drags. A horizontal swipe past a fifth of its width now moves to
  the next or previous item; a shorter one snaps back. The content does not yet
  follow the finger during the swipe (#686). Inside a `container.DirectionScope`
  whose direction differs from the app's, the swipe still pages the app's way,
  so it runs backwards there (#696).
