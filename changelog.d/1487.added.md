* **Trackpad gestures reach widgets on the desktop**
  ([#1487](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/pull/1487), part of
  [#391](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/391)). A trackpad pinch on
  macOS now reaches `Pinchable` widgets such as charts, and so does ctrl+wheel
  over one, which is how Windows precision touchpads and browsers send a pinch.
  Elsewhere ctrl+wheel still scrolls. `ScrollEvent.Phase` says whether a scroll
  starts a touchpad gesture, continues it, or is the coast after the fingers
  lift; a widget that also implements the new `event.ScrollEnder` is told when
  they lift. macOS reports all of these, Wayland all but the coast. A mouse
  wheel, and Windows and X11, report none, and their scrolls keep
  `ScrollSourceMouseWheel`.
