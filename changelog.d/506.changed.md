* **x/mmedia: the audio device is always opened at 48 kHz, and in stereo
  unless a surround stream opens it**
  ([#506](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/506)). A process has one
  audio device, and its format used to be whatever the first stream played:
  after a mono clip, every later stream in the process played in mono. Now
  the rate is always 48 kHz and the channels are stereo, except that a 5.1 or
  7.1 stream opened first without `WithDownmix` keeps its layout, as before.
  Each stream's decoder converts to that format with FFmpeg's swresample. One
  audible difference: a mono source now plays on both channels at -3 dB each,
  swresample's level for a centred source, where a mono device played it at
  full level.
