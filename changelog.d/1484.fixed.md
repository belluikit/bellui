* **x/tables: with floating filters on, a tap reaches the row tapped**
  ([#1484](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/pull/1484)). The grid measured
  body rows from the bottom of the header and left out the filter row below
  it, so every tap landed on the row below the one tapped, and screen-reader
  bounds and the keyboard context menu were one filter row off. A row scrolled
  into view from below also stopped a header height short of the body's
  bottom edge; it now comes to rest on it.
