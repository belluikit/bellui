* **x/tables: a `DataGrid` row's checkbox toggles its row**
  ([#1504](https://github.com/belluikit/bellui/issues/1504)). With
  `Options.CheckboxSelection`, a click on a row's checkbox selected the row,
  then showed the box unchecked; on a touch screen a tap on the box selected
  nothing at all. A click or tap on the box now toggles that row, as in AG
  Grid: with `SelectionMultiRow` it adds or removes the row and keeps the
  others (before, it replaced the selection), and with `SelectionSingleRow`
  it selects the row or, if it was the selected one, clears it. The box shows
  the row's state. Its accessible toggle action does the same. In a cell
  selection mode the box selects the row's first cell, as before.
